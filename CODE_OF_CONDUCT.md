# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful,
patient and constructive. Critique ideas and code, and help people participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and deliberate
disruption are unacceptable in issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or ban
accounts. Responses should be proportionate and protect people who report harm.
Maintainers follow these standards too.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact options on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sanjeev-Karthick). Do not post personal
details in a public issue. If no private contact option is available, request a
private contact channel without describing the incident publicly. A dedicated
private conduct-reporting address is a release-readiness task.

These community standards are inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
